WHAT IS KORA
ما هي كورة

KORA presents Saudi football as a complete industry, not only a sport.

Three days in Dhahran where the record of the game meets the machinery around it. KORA connects history, media, technology, investment, entrepreneurship and entertainment — and puts the future of the sport on one floor, in one square.
